# 3. Alpine Climbing, Glacier Travel & Crevasse Rescue Equipment

When ski terrain exceeds 45°–55° or crosses crevassed glaciers and exposed rocky arêtes, the player transitions from skinning to **Bootpacking, Mixed Climbing, and Roped Travel**.

## A. Crampons (Ski vs. Boot)

### 1. Ski Crampons (Harscheisen)
* **Specs:** Slide directly into a slot on the tech binding toe piece; rotate with the boot heel lift so teeth bite into bulletproof wind-scoured snow on every step.
* **Benchmarks:** ATK Race/Touring Crampons, Dynafit Crampons (80mm, 90mm, 100mm, 110mm widths).
* **Gameplay Mechanic:** Prevents side-slipping on icy traverse kick-turns without having to take skis off.

### 2. Boot Crampons (10-Point and 12-Point)
* **Ultralight Aluminum/Hybrid Skimo Crampons (290g–450g per pair):**
  * *Benchmarks:* Petzl Irvis Hybrid (steel front piece for biting hard ice + aluminum heel piece for weight savings + Dyneema cord linking system), Camp Skimo Total Race (automatic spring-loaded toe bail), Blue Ice Harfang.
  * *Visuals:* Compact foldable design with flexible white/orange UHMWPE cord linking the front and rear underfoot plates; packs into a tiny pouch the size of a burrito.
* **Technical Steel Mountaineering/Ice Crampons (750g–950g per pair):**
  * *Benchmarks:* Petzl Vasak / Sarkens / Lynx (modular mono-point or dual vertical front points), Grivel G12, Black Diamond Sabretooth.
  * *Visuals:* Chromoly steel, orange anti-balling plates underneath (prevents wet snow from building up into dangerous stilts under the boot), wire toe bail + leverlock heel clip for ski boots.

## B. Ice Axes & Technical Tools
1. **Ultralight Skimo Axe (45cm–50cm, 200g–260g):**
   * *Benchmarks:* Petzl Gully / Ride, Camp Corsa Race / Corsa Nanotech, Blue Ice Hummingbird Titanium.
   * *Visuals:* Beveled aluminum shaft (open bottom tube that fills with snow for plunging), steel or titanium pick head, sliding hand rest, short enough to stow entirely *inside* the backpack so it doesn't snag on tree branches or rocks during the ski descent.
2. **Technical Hybrid Alpine Ice Tool (45cm–50cm, 350g–500g):**
   * *Benchmarks:* Petzl Sum'Tec / Quark, Black Diamond Venom LT Tech, Grivel Ghost Evo.
   * *Visuals:* Curved shaft for clearance on steep water ice, replaceable hot-forged steel pick, interchangeable adze (for chopping ledges/snow bollards) and hammer head (for pounding pitons and pickets).

## C. Harnesses, Ropes & Crevasse/Rappel Hardware

### 1. Skimo / Glacier Harness (80g–150g)
* **Benchmarks:** Petzl Fly / Altitude, Blue Ice Choucas Pro, Arc'teryx AR-395a, Edelrid Loopo Lite.
* **Visuals & Design:** Paper-thin Dyneema webbing; **clip-open leg loops** so the player can put the harness on while wearing skis or crampons without lifting their feet off the ground; ice screw clipper slots on hip belt.

### 2. Ropes & Cord
* **RAD Line / Hyperstatic Skimo Cord (6mm, 30m–60m, ~22g/m):**
  * *Benchmarks:* Petzl RAD (Rescue And Descent) Line 6mm, Edelrid Rap Line Protect Pro Dry (dynamic reserve for one fall), Mammut Glacier Cord.
  * *Visuals:* Ultra-thin rough-sheath aramid/HMPE cord that allows micro-ascenders and friction hitches to grip reliably; packs into a small throw bag. Used for glacier travel, hauling a partner out of a crevasse, and 30m–60m ski rappels into couloirs.
* **Half / Twin Dynamic Alpine Ropes (7.5mm–8.5mm, Dry Treated):**
  * *Benchmarks:* Beal Opera 8.5mm Unicore, Mammut Alpine Sender Dry, Sterling Duetto.
  * *Function:* Full UIAA dynamic impact absorption for lead climbing rock/ice pitches above the ski line.

### 3. Hardware & Protection Rack
* **Crevasse Rescue Kit:**
  * *Petzl Micro Traxion / Nano Traxion:* Progress-capture pulley with toothed cam (essential for building a 3:1 or 6:1 Z-pulley haul system).
  * *Petzl Tibloc:* 35g emergency toothed rope clamp.
  * *Belay/Rappel Device:* Petzl Reverso, Black Diamond ATC-Alpine Guide, or Edelrid Mega Jul.
  * *Hollowblock2 / 6mm Prusik Loop:* Aramid friction hitch cord for backing up rappels with cold gloved hands.
* **Snow, Ice & Rock Anchors:**
  * *Snow Picket (50cm–60cm aluminum T-beam or Yates Cable Picket):* Driven vertically (deadman) or horizontally (T-slot) into consolidated snow for crevasse rescue or rappel anchors.
  * *Ice Screws (13cm, 16cm, 21cm):* Petzl Laser Speed Light (aluminum tube with steel drill tip and integrated folding crank handle), Blue Ice Aero Lite. Includes a V-thread (Abalakov) hook tool for threading cord through solid glacier ice to leave a zero-gear rappel anchor.
  * *Rock Protection:* Pitons (knifeblades/angles), DMM Alloy Offsets/Peenuts, Black Diamond Camalot Ultralights (#0.4–#2) for mixed rock anchors at the top of steep couloirs.
