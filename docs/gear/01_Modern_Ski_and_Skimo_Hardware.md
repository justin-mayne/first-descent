# 1. Modern Ski & Ski Mountaineering (Skimo) Hardware

## A. Skis by Discipline
In a video game context, skis dictate turn radius, float in powder, high-speed stability (chatter/dampening), and uphill skinning stamina drain (weight).

### 1. Skimo Race / Ultralight Touring
* **Specs:** 64mm–70mm waist, 150cm–165cm length, 650g–800g per ski.
* **Construction:** Paulownia wood core wrapped in full carbon fiber weave, minimal ABS sidewall, tip notch for rapid bungee skin attachment.
* **Real-World Benchmarks:** Atomic Backland UL 65, Fischer Transalp RC Carbon, Movement Race Pro 66, Ski Trab Gara Aero World Cup.
* **Visual/3D Cues:** Extremely narrow profile, exposed carbon weave textures, notched plastic tip cap, flat tail.
* **Gameplay Profile:** Maximum uphill speed and minimal stamina drain; terrible float in deep powder; twitchy and punishing at high speeds on variable chop.

### 2. Technical Ski Mountaineering / Light Touring
* **Specs:** 82mm–92mm waist, 165cm–180cm length, 1,000g–1,350g per ski.
* **Construction:** Karuba/Poplar light wood core, carbon/fiberglass stringers, titanal binding reinforcement plate.
* **Real-World Benchmarks:** Black Crows Camox Freebird, Blizzard Zero G 85/95, Dynafit Blacklight 88, Salomon MTN 86 Carbon.
* **Visual/3D Cues:** Matte textured topsheets (to shed snow), skin-clip tail notches, moderate tip rocker, flat mountaineering tails (for plunging skis vertically into snow for anchors).
* **Gameplay Profile:** The ultimate all-rounder for steep couloirs, jump turns, and multi-day traverses.

### 3. Freeride Touring / Freetour
* **Specs:** 102mm–112mm waist, 175cm–190cm length, 1,500g–1,850g per ski.
* **Construction:** Poplar/Beech core, carbon/flax dampening, partial titanal laminates, full perimeter sidewalls.
* **Real-World Benchmarks:** Atomic Bent 110, Faction La Machine, K2 Mindbender 106C, DPS Pagoda Tour CFL 105, Armada Locator 104.
* **Visual/3D Cues:** Wide shovel tips, progressive twin or rockered tails, bold graphic art.
* **Gameplay Profile:** High float in deep powder, high stability for cliff drops and high-speed big-mountain lines, higher uphill stamina penalty.

### 4. All-Mountain / Piste Carvers
* **Specs:** 80mm–98mm waist, 1,900g–2,300g per ski.
* **Construction:** Dual Titanal sheets, dense wood core, heavy elastomer dampening.
* **Real-World Benchmarks:** Volkl M7 Mantra, Nordica Enforcer 94, Blizzard Anomaly 88, Head Kore 94 Ti.
* **Visual/3D Cues:** Metallic/brushed aluminum accents, integrated binding plates, squared-off power tails.
* **Gameplay Profile:** Maximum edge hold on icy hardpack and corduroy, zero chatter at 100+ km/h, massive penalty if used for uphill touring.

## B. Bindings
Bindings govern the **Transition Time** mechanic (switching between ski and walk mode), release safety (DIN / injury prevention), and weight.

1. **Skimo Race Tech Bindings (105g–150g per binding):**
   * *Benchmarks:* ATK Revolution World Cup, Plum Race 99, Dynafit Low Tech Race.
   * *Visuals:* Skeletonized CNC-machined 7075 aluminum and titanium U-springs, no brakes (uses leash or micro-brake), single flip-over heel flap covering the pins.
   * *Game Mechanic:* 5-second transitions (can rip skins with skis still on feet!). Fixed release value (around DIN 8–9), higher knee injury risk in high-speed crashes.
2. **Free-Touring Pin Bindings (280g–380g per binding):**
   * *Benchmarks:* ATK Freeraider 15 EVO, Marker Alpinist, Dynafit Rotation, Plum Guide.
   * *Visuals:* Metallic anodized CNC body, magnetic heel risers (flat, medium, high climbing positions), retractable ski brakes.
   * *Game Mechanic:* Adjustable DIN (6–15), 2 levels of heel risers for steep skin tracks, low weight with solid downhill power transfer.
3. **Hybrid / Shift Bindings (850g–1,100g per binding):**
   * *Benchmarks:* Salomon S/Lab Shift2 13 MN, Marker Duke PT 16, Fritschi Tecton 13.
   * *Visuals:* Transformer-style toe piece that folds out to expose tech pins for uphill, then snaps into a full alpine frame toe for the descent.
   * *Game Mechanic:* Best downhill elasticity and crash safety, heavier uphill penalty, slower multi-step transition (45–60 seconds) prone to icing up in wet snow.

## C. Boots
Boots control ankle ROM (Range of Motion) when climbing/skinning and Flex Stiffness (90–140) when descending.

1. **Full Carbon Skimo Race Boots (500g–650g per boot):**
   * *Benchmarks:* Scarpa Alien 4.0, Pierre Gignoux Race 400, Atomic Backland Ultimate, La Sportiva Stratos VI.
   * *Visuals:* Exposed carbon fiber cuff and lower shell, built-in waterproof gaiter with zipper or BOA dial closure, single lever on the spine that locks cuff and tensions cables simultaneously, Vibram UFO lugged sole.
   * *Game Mechanic:* 75°–80° cuff rotation (feels like running shoes uphill, allows jogging on flat snow/rock), lower downhill dampening.
2. **2-Buckle Light Touring Boots (1,000g–1,250g per boot):**
   * *Benchmarks:* Scarpa F1 LT / Maestrale RS, Tecnica Zero G Peak Carbon, Dynafit TLT X, Fischer Travers CS.
   * *Visuals:* Grilamid shell, BOA forefoot closure + single upper buckle and power strap, external ski/walk lever on heel spine, full Vibram mountaineering sole with toe/heel welts for automatic crampons.
   * *Game Mechanic:* 60°–65° ROM, great rock/ice climbing agility, solid 110–120 downhill flex.
3. **4-Buckle Freeride Hybrid Boots (1,450g–1,800g per boot):**
   * *Benchmarks:* Tecnica Cochise 130 DYN, Lange XT3 Free, K2 Mindbender 130 BOA, Salomon Shift Pro.
   * *Visuals:* 4 heavy aluminum micro-adjustable buckles or dual BOA dials, thick thermo-formable liner, GripWalk rubber soles + tech pin inserts.
   * *Game Mechanic:* 45°–50° ROM (slower uphill stride), 130+ flex rating for charging steep spines and landing large drops.

## D. Climbing Skins, Poles & Packs
* **Climbing Skins:**
  * *100% Mohair Race Skins:* Fastest glide on snow, best cold-temperature grip, lower durability, attached with tip bungee (can be ripped without stepping out of bindings).
  * *65/35 Mohair-Nylon Mix (Pomoca Free Pro, Contour Hybrid, Black Diamond Glidelite):* Balanced grip on steep icy skin tracks and good glide.
* **Poles:**
  * *Fixed Carbon Skimo Poles (Leki Mezzalama, Swix Triac):* Extended foam touring grip (allows choking down on the pole on steep sidehills without adjusting height), butterfly race baskets.
  * *Telescopic 2-Piece Aluminum/Carbon Poles (Black Diamond Expedition,BCA Scepter):* Flick-lock adjustment (105cm–145cm), scraper grip for clearing snow off topsheets, hook tip for flipping binding heel risers.
* **Packs:**
  * *Skimo Race Pack (18L–25L, e.g., Camp Rapid Racing, Ortovox Switchback, Dynafit DNA):* Diagonal speed-carry hook system (allows stowing skis on back in 4 seconds without removing pack), dedicated lower side crampon pocket accessible with one hand, internal sleeve for shovel and probe.
  * *Avalanche Airbag Pack (30L–40L, e.g., Black Diamond JetForce Pro, Scott Patrol E2 Alpride, Ortovox Avabag LiTRIC):* Electronic supercapacitor fan system that inflates a 150L bright balloon around the head/shoulders in 3 seconds to keep the player on the surface of a moving slab avalanche.

## E. Avalanche Rescue Essentials (The Holy Trinity)
1. **Digital 3-Antenna Transceiver / Beacon (457 kHz):**
   * *Benchmarks:* Barryvox S2, Ortovox Diract Voice, Pieps Pro IPS, BCA Tracker 4.
   * *Mechanics:* Worn in chest harness. Switches from `SEND` to `SEARCH`. Displays distance (meters) and directional arrow during coarse/fine search phases.
2. **Collapsible Aluminum Avalanche Shovel:**
   * *Benchmarks:* Ortovox Pro Light, BCA Dozer 2H (Hoe-mode), Black Diamond Transfer LT.
   * *Mechanics:* UIAA-certified telescoping shaft, T-grip or D-grip, converts into 90-degree hoe mode for rapid conveyor-belt snow excavation.
3. **Quick-Lock Avalanche Probe (240cm–320cm):**
   * *Benchmarks:* Mammut Carbon Probe 280 Speed Lock, Black Diamond Quickdraw Tour.
   * *Mechanics:* Kevlar or steel internal cable with depth markings (every 5cm) to pinpoint burial depth before shoveling.
