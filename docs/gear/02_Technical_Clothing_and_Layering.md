# 2. Technical Clothing & Layering System

In modern ski mountaineering and winter backcountry games, clothing is an active **Thermoregulation & Moisture Management** puzzle. Over-dressing on a steep climb causes sweat accumulation (`Wetness`), which leads to rapid hypothermia when stopping on a windy ridge.

## A. The 4-Layer Architecture

### 1. Base Layers (Next-to-Skin Moisture Wicking)
* **Ultralight Merino Mesh / Polypropylene (120g–150g/m²):**
  * *Benchmarks:* Brynje Super Thermo Mesh, Aclima WoolNet, Odlo Active F-Dry.
  * *Function:* Traps warm air in open mesh pockets while moving sweat immediately to outer layers; ultra-fast drying.
* **Midweight Merino Wool (200g–250g/m²) & Sun Hoodies:**
  * *Benchmarks:* Icebreaker 200 Oasis, Smartwool Classic Thermal, Mons Royale Yotei, Patagonia Capilene Thermal Weight / Tropic Comfort Sun Hoody (for spring glacier touring).
  * *Function:* Natural odor resistance on multi-day trips, retains insulation even when damp.

### 2. Active Mid-Layers (Breathable Insulation for Uphill Movement)
* **Grid Fleece (Polartec Power Grid):**
  * *Benchmarks:* Patagonia R1 Air / R1 Pullover, Arc'teryx Delta, Rab Ascendor, Ortovox Fleece Grid.
  * *Visuals:* Geometric waffle/channel interior texture, chest zip pocket, thumb loops, scuba-style under-helmet hood.
* **Active Air-Permeable Synthetic Insulation (Polartec Alpha Direct / Octa / FullRange):**
  * *Benchmarks:* Patagonia Nano-Air, Arc'teryx Proton LT, Rab Xenair Alpine, Norrøna Lyngen Alpha90.
  * *Function:* High CFM (cubic feet per minute) air permeability dumps excess heat and moisture while skinning uphill so the player rarely has to stop and strip layers.

### 3. Outer Shells (Softshell vs. Hardshell)
* **Skimo Race Suit (One-Piece Lycra/Windstopper):**
  * *Benchmarks:* Crazy Idea Pull Resolution, Dynafit DNA Race Suit, La Sportiva Raceborg, Karpos.
  * *Visuals:* Form-fitting aerodynamic spandex with windproof membrane on the chest/thighs, internal chest pockets specifically sized to hold climbing skins against body heat (keeps skin glue warm and sticky in freezing weather), reinforced Kevlar kick-patches at ankles, boot-buckle cutouts.
* **Touring Softshell Jackets & Pants:**
  * *Benchmarks:* Arc'teryx Rush Softshell, Ortovox Berrino, Mammut Eigerjoch Light, Raide TourTech Bib.
  * *Function:* 4-way stretch woven nylon, wind-resistant and water-repellent (DWR), maximum breathability for dry/cold continental snowpacks.
* **3-Layer (3L) Hardshell Jacket & Bibs (GORE-TEX Pro / ePE / Dermizax):**
  * *Benchmarks:* Arc'teryx Alpha SV / Rush, Norrøna Lofoten Gore-Tex Pro, Patagonia PowSlayer, Salomon S/Lab QST, Kühl The Defiant Shell.
  * *Visuals:* Rigid weatherproof matte fabric, AquaGuard watertight zippers, massive underarm pit zips and thigh vents, helmet-compatible storm hood with laminated wire brim, integrated RECCO reflector, internal snow gaiters.
  * *Function:* 28,000mm+ waterproof rating; completely blocks blizzard wind and wet coastal snow, at the cost of lower breathability (requires opening pit-zips during climbs).

### 4. Static Insulation (Belay Parkas & Down Puffies)
* **Ultralight Packable Down Jacket (800–1000 Fill Power):**
  * *Benchmarks:* Arc'teryx Cerium, Rab Mythic Ultra, Mountain Hardwear Ghost Whisperer, Patagonia Down Sweater.
  * *Function:* Compresses into its own pocket (250g–350g); thrown on immediately during summit transitions.
* **Heavy Expedition Belay Parka (Box-Wall Down or PrimaLoft Gold Cross Core):**
  * *Benchmarks:* Patagonia Grade VII Down Parka, Arc'teryx Nuclei SV, Rab Positron Pro, Feathered Friends Khumbu.
  * *Visuals:* High-loft baffled silhouette, two-way main zipper (allows access to climbing harness belay loop while wearing the coat), oversized internal mesh drop pockets (for thawing frozen water bottles or gloves).

## B. Extremities, Footwear & Eyewear

### 1. The 3-Glove System
Ski mountaineers carry multiple pairs of gloves in their pack because wet hands mean frostbite:
* **Uphill Liner / Softshell Glove:** Lightweight windproof fleece with silicone palm grip for pole handling (e.g., Camp G Comp Wind with built-in stowable over-mitt, Black Diamond Tour).
* **Technical Work/Descent Glove:** Goat leather palm, GORE-TEX insert, articulated fingers for handling ice axes, carabiners, and ski buckles (e.g., Hestra Army Leather Patrol / Heli Ski, Black Diamond Guide Finger, Arc'teryx Fission SV).
* **Emergency Expedition Mitt:** Down or high-loft Primaloft mitten kept dry at the bottom of the pack for extreme windchill or frostbite prevention (e.g., Black Diamond Mercury Mitt, Outdoor Research Alti II).

### 2. Headwear & Optics
* **Triple-Certified Helmet (Ski + Mountaineering + Cycling — EN 1077, EN 12492, EN 1078):**
  * *Benchmarks:* Salomon MTN Lab, Petzl Meteor, Mammut Haute Route MIPS, Camp Storm, Sweet Protection Ascender.
  * *Visuals:* High-ventilation honeycomb channels, headlamp elastic clips on all four corners, ultra-low profile polycarbonate shell.
* **Glacier Glasses (Category 3–4 / Photochromic 2–4):**
  * *Benchmarks:* Julbo Explorer 2.0 / Shield (with removable leather/spandex side shields to block intense snow albedo UV reflection), Smith Pursuit, Vuarnet Glacier.
  * *Game Effect:* Prevents `Snow Blindness` (actinic keratoconjunctivitis) at high altitudes on sunny glacier days.
* **Frameless Cylindrical/Toric Ski Goggles:**
  * *Benchmarks:* Smith 4D MAG, Oakley Line Miner Pro, Sweet Protection Boondock, Julbo Aerospace (features a pop-out lens hinge that vents steam during uphill climbs).
